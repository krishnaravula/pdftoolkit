# Privacy Policy — PDF Tools - Merge, Split & Scan

Last updated: June 22, 2026

## Overview

PDF Tools - Merge, Split & Scan processes PDF and image files **locally on your device**. We do not operate a backend server for core PDF features in this version of the app.

## Data we process

- **Files you select or create** (PDFs, images, scanned pages) are read and written on your device only.
- **Recent files metadata** (file name, path, size, feature used) is stored locally in an on-device database.

## Data we do not collect (default build)

When ads and in-app purchases are **disabled** (default build configuration), the app does not initialize advertising or billing SDKs and does not send analytics to us.

## Optional features (when enabled by the developer)

If the app publisher enables **Google AdMob**, Google may collect device and ad interaction data according to [Google's policies](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Update the Google Play Data safety form accordingly.

If **Google Play Billing** is enabled, purchase transactions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — used only for document scanning when you start a scan.
- **Storage / media access** — used only to read files you pick and save outputs you create.
