## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
PDF Tools - Merge, Split & Scan is your all-in-one offline PDF utility for Android. Merge documents, split large files, compress for sharing, scan paper with your camera, convert photos to PDF, unlock password-protected files, and reorder or delete pages — all without uploading your files to the cloud.

Every tool runs locally on your device. Work on the go without Wi‑Fi and keep sensitive documents private.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
KEY FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Merge PDF — Combine multiple PDFs into one file in the order you choose.

• Split PDF — Break a PDF into smaller files. Split every N pages or export one file per page.

• Compress PDF — Reduce file size with three quality levels: Smaller, Medium, or Sharper. If compression would make the file larger, your original is kept.

• Image to PDF — Turn photos and screenshots from your gallery into a single PDF.

• Scan to PDF — Capture multi-page documents with your camera. Review each page, crop edges, and apply Color, Grayscale, or Black & White filters before exporting.

• Remove Password — Unlock password-protected PDFs when you have the correct password.

• Sign PDF — Draw, type, or import a visual signature, optionally add a date, then move and resize the marks on the page. This is a visual mark, not a certificate-based digital signature or identity verification.

• Organize Pages — Preview and select pages, drag to rearrange them, rotate or duplicate pages, insert another PDF, extract a selection, and undo changes before saving.

• Built-in PDF Viewer — Search and select text, browse page thumbnails and document outlines, jump to a page, and automatically continue where you stopped reading.

• Recent Files — Return to your latest work quickly with thumbnail previews.

• Save, Open & Share — Store results on your device and share through any app you already use.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHO IT'S FOR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PDF Tools is built for anyone who needs fast, reliable PDF tasks on Android:

• Students combining notes, assignments, and scanned handouts
• Professionals preparing contracts, invoices, and reports for email or messaging
• Small business owners digitizing receipts, forms, and signed documents
• Commuters and travelers who need offline tools without a desktop
• Privacy-conscious users who want document processing to stay on their phone or tablet

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOW TO USE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Open the app and pick a tool from the home screen.
2. Select the PDF or images you want to work with.
3. Adjust options — compression quality, page order, scan filters, and more.
4. Tap to process. A progress indicator keeps you informed.
5. Name your file, save it locally, open it in the built-in viewer, or share it instantly.

First-time users get a short onboarding walkthrough. Recent files appear on the home screen so you can pick up where you left off.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHY OFFLINE MATTERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• No account required
• No cloud uploads for core PDF processing
• Works on Android 10 and later
• Ideal for sensitive documents and spotty connections

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERMISSIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Camera — used only when you start a document scan
• Storage / media access — used only to read files you select and save outputs you create

PDF Tools is a productivity utility — not a cloud storage service. You control where your files are saved and how they are shared.

Download PDF Tools - Merge, Split & Scan and handle everyday PDF tasks in seconds — privately, offline, and right from your pocket.
```
