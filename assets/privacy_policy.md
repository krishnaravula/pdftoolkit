# Privacy Policy — PDF Tools

Last updated: November 3, 2026

## Overview

PDF Tools processes PDF and image files locally on your device. Core PDF features do not upload your documents to our servers.

## Local processing

All merge, split, compress, image-to-PDF, scan, page organization, password removal, visual signing, and viewing operations run on your device. Your PDF content and signature images are not sent to us for processing.

## What we store on your device

Android cloud backup is disabled for app-private files and settings.

- Recent files metadata: display name, file path or content URI, file size, operation type, and timestamps.
- Thumbnail images generated from the first page of recent PDFs (stored in app cache).
- Temporary working files during processing (cleared from Settings or automatically over time).
- Your preferences (theme, sort order, save location, and similar settings).
- Your last-read page, keyed by the local file path or a hash of the Android document URI.
- If you choose to save a reusable signature, its image is stored in PDF Tools' app-private storage on this device. You can delete it from the signing screen. Signatures not saved for reuse are kept only for the current signing session and output copy.

Recent-file records contain operation metadata only; they do not contain signature image bytes, PDF passwords, or annotation text. A signed PDF copy contains the visual marks you chose to place.

The signing feature adds a visual appearance to a PDF. It does not verify identity or create a certificate-based cryptographic signature.

## What we do not collect

The app does not include advertising, does not require an account, and does not send analytics or document content to us.

Third-party libraries (PDF rendering, file picking, sharing) may transmit technical diagnostics required by those SDKs. No document content is included.

## Accounts

No account is required to use PDF Tools.

## Clearing your data

From Settings you can:

- Clear temporary files (cached thumbnails and processing artifacts; permanent output files are not deleted).
- Clear recent history (removes the recent list only; does not delete your PDF files).
- Delete a saved reusable signature from the signing screen.

## Permissions

- Camera — document scanning only when you start a scan.
- Storage / media access — reading files you pick and saving outputs you create.
