# Privacy Policy — PDF Tools

Last updated: July 3, 2026

## Overview

PDF Tools processes PDF and image files locally on your device. Core PDF features do not upload your documents to our servers.

## Local processing

All merge, split, compress, image-to-PDF, scan, page reorder, password removal, and viewing operations run on your device. Your PDF content is not sent to us for processing.

## What we store on your device

- Recent files metadata: display name, file path or content URI, file size, operation type, and timestamps.
- Thumbnail images generated from the first page of recent PDFs (stored in app cache).
- Temporary working files during processing (cleared from Settings or automatically over time).
- Your preferences (theme, sort order, save location, and similar settings).

We do not store PDF passwords, signature images, or annotation text in the recent-files database.

## What we do not collect (default build)

When advertising and in-app purchases are disabled (the default configuration), the app does not initialize AdMob or Play Billing and does not send analytics to us.

## Optional SDKs (when enabled by the publisher)

If Google AdMob is enabled, Google may collect device and ad interaction data according to Google's privacy policy.

If Google Play Billing is enabled, purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.

Third-party libraries (PDF rendering, file picking, sharing) may transmit technical diagnostics required by those SDKs. No document content is included.

## Accounts

No account is required to use PDF Tools.

## Clearing your data

From Settings you can:

- Clear temporary files (cached thumbnails and processing artifacts; permanent output files are not deleted).
- Clear recent history (removes the recent list only; does not delete your PDF files).

## Permissions

- Camera — document scanning only when you start a scan.
- Storage / media access — reading files you pick and saving outputs you create.
